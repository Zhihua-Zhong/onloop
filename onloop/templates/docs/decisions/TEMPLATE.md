# Decision NNNN: short title

- **Status:** proposed | accepted | superseded by NNNN
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Decided by:** role, or the human
- **Size:** small | medium | large

## Context

What forced a decision. Facts only, with the evidence.

## Decision

What we will do, in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

What gets easier, what gets harder, and what we will watch.

## Decisions by size

Decide at the lowest level that can; the size says who.

| Size | Examples | Who decides | What the human sees |
|---|---|---|---|
| small | naming, local refactors, a tool choice inside one role | the role that owns it | nothing; it is in the changelog |
| medium | an interface between roles, a new check, a change of order | the lead | a line in the daily status |
| large | anything in a reserved class: a claim, widening safety or authority, destruction, money or accounts, anything that leaves the project | the human | a yes or no with a recommendation, once a day |

When in doubt, treat a decision as one size larger and say so.
